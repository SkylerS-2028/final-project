# Final Project: Interactive To-Do List and Planner

## Project Summary
An interactive to-do list and planner. 

## Key features and functionality 

Enter items on to-do list
 - Includes title, description, and due date on every to-do list item

 Count total hours of productivity
 - Mark how long you spent on an item and get a count of your total hours of productivity

Schedule/Planner
 - Weekly schedule where tasks can be scheduled for completion


## Instructions for use
Enter a task title, description, and due date to add a task to your page. After clicking the add button, your new task will appear, displaying all information about the task.

On the top left of your page are a count of all completed tasks, and a count of total hours of productivity.

You can keep track of the total hours spent on a task by entering your current hours spent on the task in the "current hours" field of the task. Click update to merge it with your total productivity hours. 
Note: enter total hours spent on the task, not hours added to the total of the task. 
Example: if you had already spent 2 hours working on the task and updated those hours, if you later spent two more hours working on the task, enter the total hours spent (four hours), not the hours spent in addition to those two hours (two hours).

Click the red 'x' on the right corner of the task to delete the task. (Warning: Any hours spent on a deleted task will not be counted towards your total productivity.)
Click the green checkmark on the right corner of the task to mark the task as completed. After doing so, your count of completed tasks will increase. Any hours spent working on a completed task will be included in total productivity hours. 

All tasks on your to-do list are addable to your weekly planner. Click the 'Show Scheduling Options' button to see all your tasks. Simply click on the checkmark next to the task on the day desired to add it to your daily planner. You can add the same task to as many days as desired. When finished, click the 'Hide Scheduling Options' button to hide the scheduling feature.
You will then have a planner with all tasks you plan to complete throughout the week. (Note: If a task is completed/deleted, it will be automatically removed from the planner.)
